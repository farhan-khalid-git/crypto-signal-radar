Crypto Signal Radar - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-05

Crypto Signal Radar respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or share personal data. All price monitoring and alert logic runs on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not require accounts, logins, or personal details.

How the app works

  • The app connects to Binance public WebSocket endpoints to fetch live spot price data.
  • Alert calculations are performed locally on your device.

Permissions and their purpose

  • INTERNET: Fetch live price data.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detect connectivity and auto-reconnect.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Deliver price alerts (optional).
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Show a floating alert panel (optional).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Keep monitoring active while the app runs in the background.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties.

Data retention

We do not store personal data.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version will be available at the same URL.
