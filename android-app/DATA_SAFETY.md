# Data Safety Summary - Crypto Signal Radar

Use this to fill out the Google Play Data Safety form.

## Data collection
- We do not collect, store, or share personal data.

## Data sharing
- No data is shared with third parties.

## Data types collected
- None.

## Data usage
- Not applicable (no data collected).

## Data retention
- Not applicable (no data stored).

## Security practices
- All processing happens on-device.
- The app connects to Binance public WebSocket endpoints for price data.

## Optional permissions and why they are used
- INTERNET: fetch live price data from Binance.
- ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: detect connectivity and auto-reconnect.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show price alerts (optional).
- SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: show a floating alert panel (optional).
- FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: keep streaming active while monitoring.
