## Short description (80 chars max)

## Full description
Crypto Signal Radar monitors Binance spot prices and alerts you when a coin
moves beyond your chosen thresholds. Customize alert windows from 1 minute up
to 1 month, and keep alerts visible while you use other apps.

Key features:
- Live Binance spot price streaming
- Per-window thresholds for 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 1d, 1w, 1mo
- Alert cooldown controls to reduce noise
- Optional floating panel for quick glances
- Background monitoring with foreground service

Why the overlay permission is needed:
The optional floating panel uses Android's "Display over other apps"
permission to show alerts on top of other apps. It is only used to display a
small, movable panel and never records or captures your screen.
